# Bodies 3a Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans.

**Goal:** Evolvable branching body plans, simulated physically with Rapier, driven by brain-controlled joint motors.
A kinematic mode keeps the current movement for tests and later A/B choices.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-24-bodies-3a-design.md`

**Tech:** `vendor/rapier/rapier.js` (@dimforge/rapier3d-deterministic-compat 0.21.0, inlined wasm), initialised once via top-level await in `src/sim/physics.js`.

**Global Constraints:**
- deterministic; roundtrip continues identically (Rapier snapshot in saves); schema 6
- kinematic-mode suites stay green
- physical mode is the default for new runs in the app (tests choose explicitly)

**Review Focus:**
1. Torus wrap with articulated bodies: the whole body is teleported, with no joint explosions, and terrain under the edge is continuous.
2. Creatures removed on death or god-mode kill: every Rapier body and joint is removed, with no leaks and no stale handles.
3. Body expansion: recursion and symmetry stay within `maxParts`, and degenerate genomes (0 attachments) are fine.
4. Brain output layout: a joint's motor id stays tied to its part index; outputs for missing joints are ignored.
5. NaN or exploding bodies: clamp velocities, and respawn/kill a creature whose root is NaN or far underground.

### Task 1: Physics wrapper (`src/sim/physics.js`)
- **Produces:**
  - `RAPIER` (initialised)
  - `createPhysics(terrain) -> { world, step(dt), snapshot() -> Uint8Array, restore(bytes), heightCollider }`
  - The terrain heightfield covers [−M, W+M] with M = 16 (wrapped samples).
- **Tests:**
  - a dropped box rests on the heightfield at the terrain height (inside the world and in the margin)
  - two identical runs give identical positions
  - snapshot → restore continues identically

### Task 2: Body-plan genome (`src/sim/bodyplan.js`)
- **Produces:**
  - `TEMPLATES` (worm, quadruped, biped)
  - `randomBodyPlan(rng)`, `mutateBodyPlan(plan, rate, rng)`, `cloneBodyPlan`, `bodyPlanDistance`
  - `expandBody(plan, maxParts) -> parts[] {index, parent, node, pos(local to parent), rot, size, covering, organs, gripper, joint{axis, lo, hi, strength}, mirror}`
- **Tests:**
  - every template expands with symmetric limbs mirrored
  - `maxParts` is respected under deep recursion
  - mutation stays deterministic, and parts stay within their limits
  - distance is 0 to self

### Task 3: Derived stats (`src/sim/body.js` rewrite)
- **Produces:** `deriveStats(plan)` with the existing keys (speed, turnRate, senseRange, fov, attack, defense, digest*, cold/heat,
  upkeep, maxEnergy, swim, climb, camo) plus mass, endurance, staminaRegen, dexterity, spikes, joints.
- **Legacy:** `planFromParams(params)` converts the old parameter bodies (legs, eyes, …) into a plan, so tests and helpers keep working.
- **Tests:**
  - each covering and organ has a cost and a benefit
  - dexterity needs a gripper
  - endurance grows with torso mass
  - legacy params give the same direction of effects as before (the existing body tests are ported)

### Task 4: Genome and world switch to body plans (kinematic mode)
- `genome.body` becomes a plan: `randomGenome` uses templates; mutate, distance and relatedness use plans; species avgBody uses
  summary traits (parts, limbs, grippers, fur, shell, spikes, eyes, mass, diet…); stats and charts use summary traits.
- **Tests:** all existing suites green under `locomotion: 'kinematic'`, after helper updates.

### Task 5: Physical creatures (`src/sim/creature-physics.js`)
- **Lifecycle:** build bodies and joints at spawn, remove them at death, teleport at the wrap.
- **Brain:** motor targets from brain joint outputs. Inputs: proprioception, orientation, speed, stamina, rhythm.
- **Interaction:** the mouth reaches food and creatures; stamina; buoyancy and drag in water.
- Physics state is stored in saves.
- **Tests:**
  - templates move under rhythm-driven brains (displacement > body length in 30 s)
  - deterministic
  - roundtrip identical
  - death removes bodies
  - the wrap teleport keeps the body intact

### Task 6: Viewer and stats
Segment rendering, organs, the preview from parts, endurance and dexterity in the inspector and on the Traits graph, and the dextrous checkpoint. Plus e2e.

### Task 7: Tuning and benchmarks
Physical viability, locomotion improvement and perf slow tests. Tune energy, motor strength and food. Final review, then push.
